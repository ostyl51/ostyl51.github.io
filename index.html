In developmentAndroid app · personal project

Budget Lover

An app that helps people spend less and save more. Every day it shows one number, how much is safe to spend until the next payday, and an AI advisor answers money questions using the person's own numbers.

  • Safe-to-spend after bills, savings and a buffer
  • AI advisor with memory, "what if?" planning and receipt scanning
  • Trip budgets, subscriptions and a monthly wrap-up
  • Dark mode and 15 languages, including right-to-left Arabic

02

LiveWebsite · real client

FKS Stars Motors

The website for a used and imported car dealer in Strovolos, Nicosia. Customers search the cars, compare them and book a test drive over WhatsApp or Viber. The owner adds cars and photos himself from a private admin page, with nothing to install.

  • Search and filters by make, price, year and fuel
  • Car pages with photo galleries, favourites and comparison
  • Russian, English and Greek
  • Password-protected admin with photo upload, hosted free on Cloudflare
